## 🏛️ Part 1: The 7 Thematic Virtual Panels (Cross-Episode Roundtable Councils)

The virtual panels synthesize verbatim dialogue across related episodes into thematic councils:

1. 📊 [**Panel 01: The Commercial & Revenue Management Mastermind: Net Profitability & Distribution**](virtual_panels/PANEL_01_Commercial_Alignment_Sales_vs_Revenue.md)
   * *Panelists:* Francis Purvey (Ep 01), Tamie Matthews (Ep 03), Matt Camp (Ep 07), Mike Godfrey (Ep 17), Matthew Borger (Ep 20)
   * *Core Focus:* Shifting from vanity occupancy to net profitability (Net RevPAR, GOPPAR, TRevPAR); eliminating rate day-trading; applying MLOS demand filters; and managing channel distribution costs.
2. 🤖 [**Panel 02: The AI Operating System & Intelligent Hotel Technology Council**](virtual_panels/PANEL_02_AI_Technology_and_the_Human_Touch.md)
   * *Panelists:* Francis Purvey (Ep 01), Dr. David Haberlah (Ep 05), Andrew Taylor (Ep 10), Jasmine Xie (Ep 11), Jean-Christophe Buillet (Ep 14), Heidi Gempel (Ep 18)
   * *Core Focus:* The AI Operating System, enterprise data boundaries, SLMs vs. LLMs, RAG architecture, and preserving genuine human empathy alongside autonomous operational workflows.
3. 🛡️ [**Panel 03: The Crisis Management & Brand Resilience Council**](virtual_panels/PANEL_03_Crisis_Management_Brand_Recovery_and_Resilience.md)
   * *Panelists:* Francis Purvey (Ep 01), Alexia Kalis (Ep 02), Eve Weatherburn (Ep 08), Michael Johnson (Ep 12), Jean-Christophe Buillet (Ep 14)
   * *Core Focus:* Hurricane recovery, COVID resilience, brand reputation, and emergency leadership under disruption.
4. 👥 [**Panel 04: The Modern Hotelier Leadership Council: People Over Spreadsheets & Frontline Empowerment**](virtual_panels/PANEL_04_Leadership_Culture_and_People_Over_Profit.md)
   * *Panelists:* Alexia Kalis (Ep 02), Greg Brady (Ep 06), Michael Johnson (Ep 12), Kelley Wacher (Ep 13), Andrew Turner (Ep 15), Heidi Gempel (Ep 18)
   * *Core Focus:* General Manager leadership lessons; daily 15-minute leadership habits; prioritizing employee culture (eNPS); frontline empowerment; and hiring leaders smarter than yourself.
5. 🚀 [**Panel 05: The Non-Linear Career & Boardroom Capabilities Council**](virtual_panels/PANEL_05_Non_Linear_Careers_and_Boardroom_Skills.md)
   * *Panelists:* Joaquin D'Orazio (Ep 09), Michael Johnson (Ep 12), Kelley Wacher (Ep 13), Janet McBain (Ep 16), Stephen Fraser (Ep 19)
   * *Core Focus:* Boardroom-grade skills, night audit to CEO, career pivots, and overcoming self-doubt in hospitality careers.
6. 🏗️ [**Panel 06: The Hotel Development, Precincts & Asset Strategy Council**](virtual_panels/PANEL_06_Hotel_Development_Precincts_and_Asset_Strategy.md)
   * *Panelists:* Eve Weatherburn (Ep 08), Andrew Taylor (Ep 10), Jean-Christophe Buillet (Ep 14), Andrew Turner (Ep 15)
   * *Core Focus:* Masterplanning tomorrow’s hotels: wellness as revenue, mixed-use precinct integration, adaptive reuse, strata-title economics, and owner-operator lifestyle balance.
7. 🛎️ [**Panel 07: The Frontline Guest Experience & Service Craft Council**](virtual_panels/PANEL_07_Frontline_Guest_Experience_and_Service_Craft.md)
   * *Panelists:* Alexia Kalis (Ep 02), Tracy Martin (Ep 04), Greg Brady (Ep 06), Joaquin D'Orazio (Ep 09), Jasmine Xie (Ep 11)
   * *Core Focus:* Frontline empathy, hyper-personalisation, concierge craft, service recovery, and turning high-friction guest moments into lifelong brand loyalty.
